My research develops models and methods for power system planning, market operation, and resilience. Specifically, my research directions include:

## Foundation models for power systems

Investigating how foundation models can support unit commitment and economic dispatch, including the treatment of physical and operational constraints.

## Operation and stability analysis for HVDC-integrated power systems

*UTK subcontract project with NLR*

1. **Mitigation of circular flows in HVDC-integrated systems:** Developed strategies to mitigate HVDC-related circular flows under negative generator offers, system losses, and transmission congestion.
2. **Static voltage stability:** Examining the effect of AC-HVDC circular flows on voltage stability and investigating market-clearing formulations that account for stability requirements.

## Data center planning with energy storage

Developed a two-stage stochastic bilevel investment model that incorporates energy storage and spatial and temporal workload flexibility, capturing the impacts of varying LMPs on investment strategies.

## Equity in distribution system planning

Developed DG siting and sizing models with energy affordability and vulnerability constraints; used energy burden and defined expected load shedding to assess customer impacts, respectively.

## Distribution system operation and outage recovery

Developed a hybrid data-driven and model-based method and a structural decomposition approach for network reconfiguration; co-optimized mobile power sources and network restoration after outages.
